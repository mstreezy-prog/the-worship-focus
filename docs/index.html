The Worship Focus

Organize worship songs and service plans, read ChordPro charts and MusicXML scores, and annotate your music on iPad.

Get help

Email mikes@ssefc.org for support. Include your app version, iPad model, and the steps that led to the issue. Share a sample file only if you have permission to share it.

Music Stand controls

Swipe horizontally to move through chart pages and songs. MusicXML scores scroll vertically. Tap the music to hide or show controls. Reader settings adjusts chart text size, columns, and transposition. The pencil icon opens drawing tools; Apple Pencil adds marks while fingers navigate.

Keep a backup

Use the app’s library backup and restore tools to keep a separate copy of your songs and service plans. Save exported files somewhere you can access later.
