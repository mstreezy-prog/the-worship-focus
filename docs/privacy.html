Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

The Worship Focus is an iPad app for organizing worship songs, service plans, and music annotations. No account is required.

Music and annotations Songs, imported files, service plans, reader preferences, and annotations are stored locally by the app. The app does not provide its own server or automatically upload your library to the developer.

You can choose to import, export, print, share, or save files using Apple's system services and other apps. Those actions send the selected content to the destination you choose. Device backups and storage services you use may also retain app data under their own policies.

Tracking and advertising The current app does not include advertising, account registration, or developer-operated analytics. Apple may provide diagnostic and store information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies.

Support If you contact mikes@ssefc.org, the recipient receives your email address and any information or attachments you include so they can respond. Avoid sending private song libraries unless needed to troubleshoot an issue.

Managing your data You can delete songs in the app. Delete the app to remove its local data from the device. Copies you exported or stored in backups must be managed separately through the relevant service.

Contact Questions about privacy or support: mikes@ssefc.org.
